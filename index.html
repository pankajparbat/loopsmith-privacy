Privacy Policy

Loopsmith: Ring Puzzle · Last updated 9 November 2026

This policy explains what Loopsmith: Ring Puzzle (“the game”, package com.parbatstudio.loopsmith) collects, why, and who it is shared with. The game is published by Pankaj Parbat (“we”).

The short version

We do not ask you for your name, your email address, or any other personal detail, and the game has no account to sign in to. What is collected is collected automatically by the advertising and analytics services the game uses, and it is used to show ads and to understand how the game is played.

What is collected

DataWhyCollected by
Advertising ID (a resettable identifier on your device) To select and measure ads, and to limit how often the same ad is shown Google AdMob, Unity Ads
Device and app information — device model, operating system version, language, country, app version, screen size To serve ads that work on your device, and to diagnose problems Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Google Analytics for Firebase
Approximate location, derived from your IP address (country or region level — not your street address or GPS position) To serve region-appropriate ads and to comply with regional rules Google AdMob, Unity Ads
In-game activity — levels started and finished, moves made, stars earned, how long a level took, in-game coins earned and spent, hints and boosters used, which ads were shown, settings you change To see which levels are too hard or too easy and to improve the game Google Analytics for Firebase
A randomly generated app instance identifier To count players and sessions without identifying anyone Google Analytics for Firebase
Purchase records, if you buy something in the game To deliver what you bought and to prevent fraud Google Play Billing

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card number, bank details, or billing address.

What is not collected

The game does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, calendar, microphone or camera input, precise (GPS) location, health data, or messages. It requests no runtime permissions for any of these. The only Android permissions it declares are internet access, network state, vibration (for haptic feedback), the advertising ID, and Google Play billing.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, coin balance, unlocked themes, and sound and vibration settings are saved on your device only. They are not uploaded to us and we cannot read them. Uninstalling the game deletes them permanently — there is no cloud backup and no way for us to restore them.

Who the data is shared with

We do not sell your data. It is processed by the services listed above, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy — covers AdMob, Analytics for Firebase, and Google Play Billing
  • Unity Privacy Policy — covers Unity Ads, used as an additional ad source

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. On most Android devices this is under Settings → Google → Ads, where you can also turn off ad personalisation. Doing so means you still see ads, but they are no longer tailored to you.
  • Turn off analytics by uninstalling. Removing the game stops all collection and deletes everything stored on the device.
  • Request deletion. Write to us at the address below and we will ask Google to delete the analytics data associated with your app instance. Because we hold no account for you, we may need your advertising ID to identify the right records.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

How long data is kept

Analytics data is retained by Google for the period configured in Google Analytics, which is at most 14 months for user-level records. Advertising data is retained according to Google's and Unity's own policies, linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will also be noted in the game's listing on Google Play.
